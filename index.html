Who we are

Tree work is a trade. We treat it like one.

Every job starts the same way: someone walks the site and looks at the lean, the wind, what is underneath the tree and what the client actually wants to end up with.

SGD Professional Tree Felling is a Garden Route outfit built around climbing and rigging work – the jobs where a tree cannot simply be dropped in one piece. We take sections down on ropes over roofs, pools, fences and boundary walls, then chip the brush and clear the mess on the same visit.

The lighter work matters just as much. Correct pruning keeps a tree structurally sound and looking like a tree instead of a hat-stand, and it is a great deal cheaper than removing one that has been butchered for years.

What we do

Everything from a single branch to a cleared plot.

One crew, one set of machinery, one invoice – including the chipping and the load-out that most quotes leave for someone else.

01

Large Tree Felling

Straight fells where there is room, sectional dismantling where there is not. Gums, pines and old garden giants.

02

Pruning & Trimming

Crown thinning, deadwooding, clearance from roofs and gutters, shaping – cut at the collar so the wound closes properly.

03

Stump Removal

Stumps ground out below grade so you can lawn, pave or plant over the spot instead of working around it.

04

Aerial Rigging

Rope and lowering work over roofs, pools and boundary walls. Nothing free-falls where it can do damage.

05

Wood Chipping & Chipper Hire

Brush chipped on site and removed, or keep the chips for mulch. Chipper hire available for your own clearing jobs.

06

Site & Plot Clearing

Overgrown erven, invasive stands and building sites cleared, stacked and hauled away ready for the builders.

07

Live Edge Slabbing

Good timber milled into live edge slabs rather than burnt – tables, counters and mantels out of your own tree.

08

Emergency Tree Work

Trees on roofs, split limbs and storm damage. Call any hour – we make it safe first, then deal with the clean-up.

09

Removal & Clean-Up

Timber, brush and debris loaded and taken off site. We rake down before we leave – that part is not an extra.

Why SGD

The difference is what happens before the saw starts.

Anyone can drop a tree in an open field. The skill is in the ones surrounded by things that must not get hit.

  • Certified arborists, fully insured. Cover in place before we set foot on your property, and we will show it to you.
  • Rigged, not dropped. Sections lowered on ropes where roofs, glass, pools or powerlines are in play.
  • Proper PPE and rope access. Helmets, chaps, climbing systems and cut-resistant kit on every climber, every job.
  • Own chipper and truck. No waiting on a second contractor to clear what we cut.
  • A site left clean. Chipped, raked and swept – you should not be able to tell where the brush was stacked.
  • Straight pricing. A written quote after a site visit, with nothing hidden underneath it.

Tree down after a storm? Emergency callouts are answered around the clock on 064 129 4129. If it is on a roof or a line, phone rather than message.
